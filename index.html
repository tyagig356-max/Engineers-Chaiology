Our Story · Built with an engineer’s mindset

From Engineer
to Entrepreneur.

Behind Engineer’s Chaiology is the entrepreneurial spirit of Er. Sachin Tyagi — a name that connects engineering curiosity with a love for bringing people together over great chai and food.

An engineer sees possibilities and builds solutions. An entrepreneur turns an idea into an experience. Engineer’s Chaiology brings those two mindsets together: a welcoming café where conversations flow, creativity brews, and every break can spark something new.

With a main outlet in Bhopal and a second outlet in Gurdaspur, the brand’s story continues, one cup and one community at a time.
